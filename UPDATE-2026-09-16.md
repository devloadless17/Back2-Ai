# Product update — 16 September 2026

Three days of work, summarised for someone who does not read the code.

---

## 1. The tutor now understands how Lebanese students actually type

**This is the headline.** Lebanese students write Arabic in Latin letters —
*arabizi* — constantly. `shu ya3ne el isti3ara w kif bfar2a 3an el tashbih` is a
perfectly good question about a real chapter of the Arabic literature syllabus.

Until today the product **refused it.** Every question typed that way was
refused, in every Arabic subject.

The cause was not translation. The system translated it correctly into
`ما معنى الاستعارة وكيف أفرّق بينها وبين التشبيه؟` and found the right passage.
It then applied the wrong safety threshold — the one meant for French and
English — and rejected its own correct answer by nine thousandths of a point.

Fixed. The same question is now answered. Off-syllabus questions are still
refused, which was checked, not assumed.

**Why it matters commercially:** no competitor product built outside Lebanon
handles arabizi, because nobody outside Lebanon knows students write that way.
It is cheap to demonstrate and impossible to fake.

---

## 2. Arabic retrieval improved by 16 points — by deleting code

The system was re-ordering Arabic search results with an AI model, justified by
a note in the code claiming it nearly doubled accuracy.

Re-measured against the full test set, it was doing the **opposite**:

| | Arabic accuracy |
|---|---|
| with the re-ordering (what shipped) | 37% |
| without it | **53%** |

It was also paying for an AI call on every Arabic question to make Arabic worse.
Removed. We then tested the expensive model on the same job across all 59 test
questions: **identical to doing nothing**, at fifteen times the cost. That line
of work is closed, with evidence.

---

## 3. Students were being billed for discounts we already received

Our AI provider automatically discounts repeated content by 90%. The billing
code had a column for this, a price for it, and the arithmetic to use it — and
never read the number. Every discounted token was charged to the student's
budget at full price.

Fixed on both providers, with tests, because the two report the figure in
opposite directions and getting it backwards is invisible in everything except
the invoice.

---

## 4. 320 chapters that students could not reach

The chapter index greyed out and disabled any chapter with no practice
questions — 320 of 1,193. But those chapters **hold the textbook**: 782 passages
sat behind ten disabled rows in LH English alone.

The chapter page had already been built to handle this — it offers the reading,
the tutor, and the nearest chapter you *can* practise. The index was the only
thing standing between students and it.

Now reachable. Only the 22 chapters with genuinely nothing behind them stay
disabled.

---

## 5. Chapter question counts were understated by 3–5×

GS Chemistry displayed 69 questions; practice actually served 352. Mathematics
showed 157 against 513. A student could reasonably have concluded the subject
was empty and stopped.

The index counted where a question was *filed* rather than which chapters may
*serve* it. Corrected in four places, including "programme covered" on the
dashboard — which, being a divisor, was **overstating** progress by about a
third.

---

## 6. What we now know that we did not know on Friday

Measured, not estimated:

- **Retrieval is healthy.** The right passage reaches the tutor **95% of the
  time** (Arabic 92%, English 97%, French 96%). This was free to measure and
  had never been checked.
- **The database is not a bottleneck.** Chapter list 13ms, search 7–9ms.
- **Summary quality is worth its cost.** The cheap model produces 5 key points
  where the current one produces 16. Tested, kept.

## The next real problem, with evidence

The tutor applies **two** safety thresholds across **seventy** subjects. Real
student questions sit in wildly different places depending on the subject:

| subject | where real questions score | refused by the gate |
|---|---|---|
| GS جغرافيا | 0.331 | **67%** |
| LS Physics | 0.468 | **50%** |
| LH فلسفة عامة | 0.374 | **30%** |
| GS Chimie | 0.713 | 0% |
| GS Physique | 0.716 | 0% |

In geography the product refuses **two of every three real questions**. In
chemistry the same threshold does nothing at all.

Per-subject calibration is the single largest available improvement, it is
measurable with the tooling built this week, and it costs nothing to run.

---

## Still open, and honest about it

- **Answer quality is unmeasured.** 26 of 4,798 questions have been checked by a
  human. Retrieval is proven; whether the answers are *good* is not. This needs
  a person marking, not more engineering.
- **320 chapters have no exam questions** because Lebanese examiners never set
  any on them — 150 have nothing within reach at any threshold. More past papers
  would fix this; software cannot.
- **Production email is not configured**, and the seeded admin password is still
  the default one.
