The LH general-philosophy book. Its contents page is the RTL two-column layout
this corpus keeps producing: a column of thirty page numbers in Arabic-Indic
digits read out first, then the titles underneath, so no entry can be paired
with its own page. The automatic parse found FIVE chapters in a 361-page book

  الوعي واللاوعي     38-95     58 pages
  اللغة والفكر       95-96      1 page     <- 46 questions
  الحقيقة            96-268   173 pages
  الله والإنسان     268-297
  الخير والقيم      297-361

Twenty-eight lessons are printed on that page. The three it missed between
الوعي واللاوعي and اللغة والفكر are the reason the latter got a single page:
with nothing between them to stop it, each located title ran to the next one it
could find.

اللغة والفكر is the most-asked chapter in LH philosophy — 46 past-exam
questions — and with two passages behind it, retrieval could never reach it.
Labelling a question filed there showed the failure directly: the tool offered
six candidate chapters and the chapter the question actually belongs to was not
among them, because it has no material to be ranked on.

Written as المحور / الدرس, which is a transcription convention for the existing
Arabic parser and not the book's own wording — it prints its parts as
"القسم الاول: فلسفة عامة" with numbered headings underneath. The lesson titles
themselves are the book's, unchanged.

Page numbers are left out deliberately, as in falsafa-gsls: the printed column
cannot be paired with entries with any confidence, and a wrong printed page
poisons the offset vote for the whole book. Each lesson opens with its own
title inside the book, which places it exactly.

تمهيد (ما هي الفلسفة؟) and مقدمة عامة (الفلسفة اليونانية) are not lessons. They
are the book's preface and its introduction to Greek philosophy, before the
first part, and are left out.
