The contents page prints its four parts as Roman numerals — I تمهيد, II الانسان,
III المعرفة, IV مسائل في الاخلاق — with the lesson titles listed bare underneath
and the page numbers in a separate column of Arabic-Indic digits down the side of
the page. OCR reads that column first and the entries afterwards, so no entry can
be paired with its page number, and the parser has no label to key on: this book
writes neither محور nor الفصل nor الدرس.

The parts and lessons are therefore written here as المحور / الدرس. That is a
transcription convention so the existing Arabic parser can read the list — it is
not the book's own wording, which uses the Roman numerals above. The lesson
titles themselves are the book's, unchanged.

Page numbers are left out deliberately. The printed column cannot be matched to
entries with any confidence, and each lesson opens with its own title inside the
book, which places it exactly.

تمهيد is not listed as a lesson. It is the book's preface, before the first part.
-->
