## III- (4 points)

In the complex plane referred to a direct orthonormal system $(O;\vec{u},\vec{v})$, consider the points $M$ and $M'$ with respective affixes $z$ and $z'$ such that $z' = (1+i)\,\bar{z}$.

**1)** In this part, let $z = e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}}$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**a-** Write $z'$ in exponential form.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**b-** Verify that $(z')^{6}$ is pure imaginary.

**2)** **a-** Show that $|z'| = \sqrt{2}\,|z|$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**b-** Deduce that, when $M$ moves on the circle with center $O$ and radius $\sqrt{2}$, $M'$ moves on a circle whose center and radius are to be determined.

**3)** Let $z = x + iy$ and $z' = x' + iy'$, where $x$, $y$, $x'$ and $y'$ are real numbers.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**a-** Express $x'$ and $y'$ in terms of $x$ and $y$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**b-** For all $z \neq 0$, denote by $N$ the point with affix $\bar{z}$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prove that the triangle $ONM'$ is right isosceles with principal vertex $N$.
