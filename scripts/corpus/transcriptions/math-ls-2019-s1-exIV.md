## IV- (8 points)

Consider the function $f$ defined over $\left]0,+\infty\right[$ as $f(x) = 2x\left(1 - \ln x\right)$. Denote by $(C)$ its representative curve in an orthonormal system $(O;\vec{i},\vec{j})$.

**1)** Determine $\lim\limits_{\substack{x \to 0 \\ x > 0}} f(x)$ and $\lim\limits_{x \to +\infty} f(x)$.

**2)** **a-** Let $A$ be the point of intersection of $(C)$ with the x-axis. Determine the coordinates of $A$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**b-** Show that $f'(x) = -2\ln x$ and set up the table of variations of $f$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**c-** Determine an equation of the tangent $(T)$ to $(C)$ at $A$.

**In the adjacent figure:**

- $(C)$ is the representative curve of $f$
- $(T)$ is the tangent to $(C)$ at $A$
- $(d)$ is the line with equation $x = 1$
- $B\left(1,\; 2e-2\right)$ is the point of intersection of $(d)$ and $(T)$.

**3)** **a-** Show that $f$ has, over $\left]1,+\infty\right[$, an inverse function $g$ whose domain of definition is to be determined.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**b-** Set up the table of variation of $g$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**c-** Copy $(C)$, then draw $(C')$, the representative curve of $g$ in the same system.

**4)** **a-** Using integration by parts, determine $\displaystyle\int x\ln(x)\,dx$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**b-** Show that $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{e} f(x)\,dx = \frac{e^{2}-3}{2}$.

&nbsp;&nbsp;**c-** Calculate the area of the shaded region bounded by $(C)$, $(T)$ and $(d)$.
